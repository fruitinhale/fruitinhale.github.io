Poppin Fruits Support

Contact

For questions, bug reports or feedback, email us at:

poppin_support@fruitinhale.com

It may take a few days for us to reply.

Please include

  • Your device and iOS version (for example, iPhone 13, iOS 18)
  • What you did and what happened
  • A screenshot, if possible

Ads

If you see an inappropriate ad, tell us the type of ad (banner, video, etc.), when it appeared, and on which screen. You can also use "Report an ad" in the app's settings (the app version is added automatically).

Your data

Your game data is stored only on your iPhone. You can delete it with "Reset data" in the app's settings, and it is also deleted when you delete the app.

For details, see our Privacy Policy:

https://fruitinhale.github.io/poppin-fruits/privacy/en/

Developer

fruitinhale (individual developer)

We will tell you the Developer's full name and address without delay if you ask.
