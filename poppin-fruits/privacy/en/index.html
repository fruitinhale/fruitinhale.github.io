Poppin Fruits Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026
Last revised: November 7, 2026

On November 7, 2026, we revised sections 2, 3 and 6.

fruitinhale (an individual developer; the "Developer") sets out how user information is handled in the smartphone game "Poppin Fruits" (the "App") as follows. The Japanese version of this policy is the official version.

1. Developer

Name: fruitinhale (individual developer)

Contact: poppin_support@fruitinhale.com

We will tell you the Developer's full name and address without delay if you ask.

2. Information kept on your device

Your game progress (cleared stages and stars, the number of lives, items and tickets, settings, etc.) is stored on your device and is not sent to the Developer.

The "lives full" notice is scheduled on your device. No information is sent for the notice.

On Android, the App asks for your birth year and month the first time you open it. Your answer is stored only on your device and is not sent to the Developer. It is used to decide how ads are handled (see 4) and the initial text style (easy text or normal text). If you are 16 or older, the App also asks whether you agree to "Ads picked for you", and that answer is also stored on your device.

3. Usage analytics

The App does not currently send usage data to the Developer. If we start sending usage data, we will update this policy, let you know in advance and ask for your permission.

If you turn on "Don't send usage data" in Settings, no usage data will be sent even if we start sending it later.

4. Ads

The App shows ads (banner ads, video ads, etc.) using Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. AdMob may collect the following information to show ads, measure ad performance, for analytics, to prevent fraud and to find and fix problems. Google may share information with other companies to deliver ads (see Google's pages below):

  • Identifiers for your device or app, such as the advertising ID
  • IP address (may be used to estimate your general location)
  • Information about ads shown and how you interact with them, and app activity such as app launches
  • App performance and crash information

Ad content is limited to content suitable for all ages, regardless of age.

iPhone: The App does not ask for permission to track, so the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used. Only non-personalized ads are shown.

Android: Based on the birth year and month you gave the first time you opened the App, ads are handled as follows. Each ad request sent to Google includes a setting based on your age and your "Ads picked for you" answer (whether it is treated as directed to children, and whether ads are non-personalized). Your birth year and month themselves are not sent.

  • Age 16 and older: Google AdMob shows ads using the advertising ID and similar information (to show ads, measure performance and prevent fraud). If you agreed to "Ads picked for you", you may also see ads based on your interests. If you have not agreed, only non-personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice in Settings at any time. Google may still show non-personalized ads at its discretion.
  • Age 13 to 15: Only non-personalized ads are shown, using the advertising ID and similar information (to show ads, measure performance and prevent fraud).
  • Under 13: Ads are shown with child-directed treatment. The advertising ID is not used and ads based on interests are not shown. Full-screen ads and video ads that give items are not shown.

You can delete or reset the advertising ID in your Android settings.

For how Google handles information, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

5. Information sent to third parties (disclosure under Japan's Telecommunications Business Act)

The App sends information to the following company:

  • Recipient: Google LLC (Google AdMob)
  • Information: the information in 4. On Android, also the setting based on your age and your "Ads picked for you" answer (whether it is treated as directed to children, and whether ads are non-personalized)
  • Purpose: showing ads, measuring performance, analytics, preventing fraud and finding and fixing problems. On Android, for users 16 and older who agreed, showing ads based on interests

We require the recipient to protect information at least as well as this policy.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Information on your device (2) is deleted when you delete the App. However, it may be included in your device backup (such as Google or iCloud) and may come back when you reinstall the App (backups are not sent to the Developer).
  • Information collected by AdMob can be managed on Google's pages above.
  • To delete your emails to us or to ask questions, contact poppin_support@fruitinhale.com. Your email address and message are used only to reply and are deleted 6 months after we reply, or sooner if you ask. Information on your device does not reach the Developer, so we cannot delete it. "Reset data" in the App deletes your progress and other records, but some information, such as your birth year and month in the Android version, stays. To delete everything, delete the App (for backups, see the first line of this section).
  • To ask us to tell you the purpose of use, or to disclose, correct, add to, delete, stop using, erase or stop sharing information we hold about you (such as your emails to us), contact us by email. We will respond without delay after confirming your identity.
  • Emails sent to us are kept in an email account protected by two-step verification, and no one other than the Developer can see them. The email account uses a service provided by Google LLC in the United States (Google Workspace).

7. Children

The App can be played at any age. The iPhone version is not listed in the Kids category. The Android version is aimed at age groups that include children, and shows ads with child-directed treatment to users under 13 (see 4). If you are under 18, please read this policy with a parent or guardian.

8. Changes

We will announce any changes to this policy on this page.

9. Contact

For questions or complaints, contact:

poppin_support@fruitinhale.com
